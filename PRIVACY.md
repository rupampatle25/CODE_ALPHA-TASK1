# Privacy Policy (Draft for Review)

**Last Updated:** September 2026

*Note: This is a practical, transparency-oriented policy draft designed for BhashaSetu. It should be reviewed by legal counsel before commercial deployment.*

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Data:** When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and encrypted password hash.
- **Translation Data:** If you are logged in, we retain your source text, translated text, language pairs, and timestamps in your personal history table.
- **Usage Metrics:** We record character usage count per billing cycle to enforce plan limits.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To deliver real-time translations via our server-side API.
- To display your personal translation history in your dashboard.
- To enforce character quotas per subscription tier.
- We do **not** sell your personal data or use your private translations to train third-party AI models.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
You can delete individual translation history records or clear your complete history directly from your dashboard at any time. If you request account deletion, your user record and all associated translations are purged from the database.
